# Team decisions

- Authenticated users can act as learners. An active tutor profile adds tutor capabilities; it does not remove learner capabilities.
- University membership is optional and self-declared in V1. Do not describe it as verified.
- Tutor profiles are DRAFT until biography, subject and availability requirements are met, then become ACTIVE when explicitly published.
- Requests and counter-proposals do not reserve time. Acceptance checks overlapping UPCOMING sessions atomically.
- Tutor absence reports and contested attendance open a dispute. Only an administrator resolves disputes with a recorded decision.
- Standard reviews are allowed only after a session is HELD.
- V1 advanced feature: in-app notifications. Real-time chat and external notification delivery are out of scope.
- Final project design document is written in English.
